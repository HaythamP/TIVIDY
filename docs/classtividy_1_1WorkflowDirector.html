Detailed Description

template<typename T>
class tividy::WorkflowDirector< T >

Director of the Builder pattern: knows the recipes of the two concrete scenarios and drives any Builder.

Incident Response (Power Outage), 5 ids: Dispatch Team, Investigation (audited), Containment, Repair (audited, escalation, approval), Restore. Job Application (Tech Company), 7 ids: group Initial Screening (Application Review and Reference Check, both audited), group Interview (Technical and Behavioural Interview, both audited), Final Decision (approval). Ids are consumed in the order the components are listed (a group id before its children).
