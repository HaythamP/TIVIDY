Detailed Description

template<typename T>
class tividy::AssignCommand< T >

ConcreteCommand: assigns participants to a work item and installs its handler chains.

Two construction forms exist. Strategy form: the candidates are always the entire participant pool of the given workflow and the strategies pick from it (no separate participant list). Direct form: no strategies; the caller supplies the participants (and optionally numAssignees to take the first few). Chains: items with EscalatedWork need an escalation chain (LevelHandlers followed by a FallbackHandler); items with ApprovedWork need an approval chain with at least one approver. Chains are never filtered by strategies. The command owns the chains until they are installed, after which the decorator owns them; an undo removes them again.
