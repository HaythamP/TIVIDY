Detailed Description

template<typename T>
class tividy::WorkflowDefinition< T >

ConcretePrototype, Product of the Builder pattern and ObjectStructure of the Visitor pattern: a complete workflow.

It holds one ordered list of top-level steps (WorkItems, decorated items or WorkGroups; steps run one after another, children of a group in parallel), the pool of Participants, the Mediator and the CommandHistory. It owns the steps, the mediator and the history but not the participants. Typical use: build a template once with a Builder, clone() it for every run, add participants, start(), then execute commands.
