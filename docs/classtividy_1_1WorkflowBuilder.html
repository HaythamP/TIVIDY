Detailed Description

template<typename T>
class tividy::WorkflowBuilder< T >

ConcreteBuilder: assembles a WorkflowDefinition step by step and returns it from getResult().

Decorators are applied in a fixed order (Audited outermost, then Escalated, then Approved, then the item) whatever order the with...() calls were made in. Ids must be unique. It owns the product until getResult() hands it to the caller, and owns the pending item until endWorkItem().

Exceptions
DuplicateIdExceptionfor a repeated id.
InvalidWorkflowExceptionfor misuse (unfinished group or item, empty group or workflow).
